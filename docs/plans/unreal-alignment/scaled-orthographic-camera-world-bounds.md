# A scaled orthographic Scene camera can cull visible mesh geometry

**Problem-only Draft.** This concerns the ordinary CPU mesh frustum test for an authored orthographic Scene camera whose inherited scale is representable as TRS. It does not change camera optics. No new ZE test, hardware result for this case, or production patch is included.

1. **Reachable difference.** Scene camera assets can author [orthographic projection, size and clip distances](../../../zircon_runtime/src/asset/assets/scene/camera.rs#L18-L32). With no explicit host camera override the [prepared World producer chooses a live Camera entity](../../../zircon_runtime/src/scene/world/render.rs#L477-L509), [publishes that camera's derived transform](../../../zircon_runtime/src/scene/world/render.rs#L604-L628), and keeps the independent lens settings. The renderer [multiplies the projection by the camera view](../../../zircon_runtime/src/core/framework/render/view_matrix_pair.rs#L12-L20), whose math currently [inverts the whole represented TRS transform](../../../zircon_runtime/crates/zr_math/src/transform.rs#L193-L210). The [CPU mesh culler](../../../zircon_runtime/src/graphics/visibility/culling/is_mesh_visible.rs#L26-L77) uses that inverse for a sphere's center but adds its unchanged [world-space radius](../../../zircon_runtime/src/graphics/visibility/declarations/visibility_bounds.rs#L1-L8) to the view-space plane inequalities.
2. **User effect and independent witness.** Attach an identity orthographic camera under `S(0.25, 1, 1)`, keep the square camera's half-height/width at `1`, and place a unit cube scaled uniformly by `0.2` at world `(0.32, 0, -5)`. The world-space right edge is `0.25`; the cube starts at `0.22`, so a visible fraction must remain. Its finite world sphere has radius `0.1√3≈0.173205`. The existing formula compares `1.28 > 1.173205` and predicts discarding the entire mesh. The same plane's world signed distance is `0.07`, less than the radius, or equivalently the view X extent is `4r≈0.692820`. An independent near-boundary witness uses camera `Sz=0.25`, near `1`, and world center Z `−0.2`, radius `0.1`: view depth `0.8`, old reach `0.9`, actual directional reach `1.2`. The pinned [Three.js r180 camera view](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/0af9729d0c143a86a1d725d6e2c3ad83301f3f34/src/cameras/Camera.js) and [world-plane frustum](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/0af9729d0c143a86a1d725d6e2c3ad83301f3f34/src/math/Frustum.js) give the expected geometry; front-face rays at sixteen pixel centers hit the cube, not ZE GPU pixels. No inherited shear, mirrored winding, projection override or unit-camera perspective normalization is necessary to reproduce the formula error.
3. **Local Unreal comparison.** `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/Camera/CameraComponent.cpp:449–473` copies component position/rotation and separate lens data; `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/SceneView.cpp:666–692` initializes a rotation-based view and its projection. This is not the same scale policy and does not authorize copying `FTransform`. The transferable contract is the world-space culling math: `SceneView.cpp:796–808` builds its convex volume from the resolved VP, `Engine/Source/Runtime/Core/Public/Math/Matrix.inl:755–766,794–836` normalizes extracted plane coefficients, and `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/ConvexVolume.cpp:310–347` compares the signed plane distance with a world radius; `Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/SceneVisibility.cpp:604–627` feeds world bounds. Applying this invariant to ZE's currently rendered view, not inheriting UE's optical policy, is the narrow comparison.
4. **Scope and potential Rust seam.** No lens, hierarchy, camera-affine-versus-rigid policy, ECS, DTO, GPU instance ABI, or sign/winding change. For the existing ordinary orthographic inequalities, a world sphere of radius `r` has support `r*length(Aᵀ n)` where `A` is the linear part of `view_from_world` and `n` is each view-plane normal. For the axis-aligned view slab this is one scale per inverse-matrix row, computed with the frame's existing camera owner, not per mesh, or equivalent normalized world planes. Preserve a unit-view and far-out control; do not use an arbitrary oversized sphere or drop culling. [#138](https://github.com/He-Jiahui/ZirconEngine/pull/138) independently corrects non-unit *perspective side normals* at unit camera scale; its three hunks touch this owner but leave orthographic checks unchanged. Keep that delivery separate. [#135](https://github.com/He-Jiahui/ZirconEngine/pull/135) fixes *object* bounds/model matrix authority, not the camera-space support of an already conservative world sphere. Large-population static prefilter, virtual geometry, shadow views, Editor rays/gizmo, scaled perspective, and singular or explicitly overridden cameras are separate qualification; don't fold them into this bounded first patch.
5. **First frozen proof.** Use the actual Level/World Camera component with `request.camera=None`, correct affinity and published matrix, ordinary rigid mesh asset/typed local bounds, a far-out cube, and a unit-camera control. On CPU, run the production visibility path with finite prepared bounds and verify the actual visible stable keys and independently check that the sampled rays intersect the authored mesh. Stay below the [static prefilter threshold](../../../zircon_runtime/src/graphics/visibility/context/history/construct.rs#L288-L356) to isolate this stage. For DX12, warm real resource admission and require prepared local bounds (no fail-open sentinel), single camera and no HZB, fixed lighting/exposure/output size, current frame generation, real central and outside controls, and compare color against the unchanged background in the right-edge patch of a 256² output, starting `(246,126)`. Existing tests using clear-only split-screen colors are not a scaled Scene-camera witness. Require old unchanged production RED before applying a fix; reuse identical selectors on the candidate and do not claim packaged acceptance or passed required checks.
