## Scope Delivered

- The material contract now carries normalized clearcoat, anisotropy, specular/diffuse transmission, thickness, IOR, attenuation, and optional clearcoat-normal data from assets through extraction, material variants, pipeline keys, shader prewarm, and the standard PBR surface ABI.
- The shader template registers `zr_pbr_extras.wgsl` and evaluates GGX, anisotropic GGX, clearcoat energy compensation, transmission BTDF, attenuation, and screen-space scene-color transmission without changing the default-material variant key.
- Transmission uses the fixed queue 2900 late-forward opaque path. The frame graph owns a physical single-sample `Rgba16Float` scene-color copy and emits ordered `scene_copy -> transmission-mesh` pairs for zero through four quality steps.
- Zero-step transmission binds a transparent-black availability sentinel and selects environment lighting in WGSL; copied scene color is selected only when the sampled alpha marks it available.
- Effective material extraction retains a renderable child's advanced features when a parent reference cannot be resolved or loaded, while still merging every valid ancestor reached before that boundary.
- Mesh command routing keeps advanced opaque and transmission lists separate; each transmission step consumes one non-overlapping depth-sorted command range and cannot be merged into a cross-step indirect batch.
- The Render18 growth-triggered owner split is atomic with this milestone: GPU mesh recording, mesh cache/advanced tests, sprite-stage selection, and shared pipeline-resource usage are mounted as child modules in the same commit as their root references.
- The three-sphere DX12 product renders clearcoat, anisotropy, and glass side by side, asserts the executor order, and verifies a visible pixel delta in every material region.
